St. Cecilia Choir HTCC Naze — Official Constitution and By-Laws

Preamble

We, the members of the St. Cecilia Choir, Holy Trinity Catholic Church (HTCC) Naze, Owerri North, Imo State, Nigeria, in our commitment to excellence in sacred music and service to God and the parish community, do hereby establish and adopt this Constitution and By-Laws to govern our activities, operations, and fellowship.

Article I — Name

The name of this organization shall be St. Cecilia Choir, Holy Trinity Catholic Church (HTCC) Naze.

Article II — Vision

To be a catalyst for encouraging and nurturing hope and uplifting faith in the Catholic Church, through good and inspiring music.

Article III — Mission

To dedicate ourselves to the excellent performance of quality music to bring hope and joy to our listeners and parishioners, fostering a spirit of harmony, discipline, and devotion among all members.

Article IV — Membership

Membership is open to all baptized Catholics who are willing to adhere to the teachings of the Catholic Church, possess a genuine love for sacred music, and commit to the ideals of the choir.

  • New members shall undergo a probationary period of three (3) months.
  • Full membership is granted upon successful completion of probation and approval by the executive committee.
  • Members are expected to attend rehearsals regularly and participate in all choir activities.
  • Membership may be revoked for persistent absenteeism, misconduct, or violation of these by-laws.

Article V — Rehearsals

Regular rehearsals are held on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, and Saturdays at 5:00 PM WAT at the Holy Trinity Catholic Church (HTCC) Naze.

  • Members are required to be punctual and prepared for every rehearsal.
  • The Choir Master/Mistress shall preside over all rehearsals.
  • Special rehearsals may be called as needed before feasts, competitions, or performances.

Article VI — Executive Committee

The executive committee shall consist of elected officers responsible for the overall management and governance of the choir.

  • President
  • Vice President
  • Secretary
  • Assistant Secretary
  • Financial Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Public Relations Officer (PRO)
  • Choir Master
  • Choir Mistress
  • Welfare Officer

Elections shall be held every two (2) years by a simple majority vote of the general membership.

Article VII — Finance

The choir shall be financed through membership dues, donations, offerings, and fundraising activities.

  • All funds shall be kept in a dedicated choir account with First Bank.
  • The Treasurer shall maintain accurate records and present financial reports at every general meeting.
  • No expenditure shall be made without the approval of the President and at least two (2) executive members.

Article VIII — Discipline

Every member is expected to uphold the dignity, decorum, and spiritual standards of the choir.

  • Tardiness to rehearsals or performances shall be addressed with a warning, then a fine after three (3) occurrences.
  • Absence from scheduled performances without valid excuse may result in suspension.
  • Misconduct, insubordination, or behavior unbecoming of a choir member shall be referred to the executive committee for appropriate action.

Article IX — Amendment

This Constitution and By-Laws may be amended by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the general membership at a specially convened meeting, provided that the proposed amendment has been circulated to all members at least seven (7) days before the meeting.

Declaration

"Soli Deo Gloria" — To God Alone Be the Glory. We, the members of St. Cecilia Choir, do solemnly pledge to uphold this Constitution and By-Laws, to serve the parish with excellence, and to honor the patronage of our beloved St. Cecilia, the patron saint of musicians.

St. Cecilia, Pray for Us. Amen.
